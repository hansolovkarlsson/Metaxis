## Function-like macros, and the empty turn that was not one

Step 5 cost the tool nothing the plan had listed, and one thing it had not.
A function-like macro remembers its body under `fn:` and each parameter
under its position; a call binds each argument to its parameter's name in
the store and expands the body, so parameter substitution is not a
`replace` over the body, which the roadmap had guessed at and which
`replace` could not do whole-token, but a lookup the identifier rule makes

same name: `TWICE(x)` is `(DOUBLE(x) + x)`, and the inner call's arguments
are expanded at the outer depth before its template runs, its own bindings

eager hole expansion without a line of C.
